# Security policy

Greenwich Utility Watch is a small civic site. If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately rather than opening a public issue.

- **Email:** contact@greenwichutilitywatch.org, subject line starting with `SECURITY`.
- **What to include:** the affected page or endpoint, steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has.
- **What to expect:** an acknowledgement within 3 business days and a fix or mitigation as quickly as the issue warrants. Please give us a reasonable window before public disclosure.

In scope: anything served from greenwichutilitywatch.org, including the submission form, gallery, dashboard, feeds and API routes.
Out of scope: denial-of-service, volume-based testing against the live site, and social engineering.

Submitter contact details are never published; see the privacy note on the submission form.
